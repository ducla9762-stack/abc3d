Shapes and How to Use Them

Cube

8 vertices and 6 faces. The classic that everyone loves, it doesn't get much simpler than this

Pyramid

5 Vertices and 5 faces. Don't let the ancient Egyptians see how easily you can put one in your scene

Ico Sphere

A lower-resolution sphere with a set 12 vertices and 19 faces

UV Sphere

A sphere that you can change the resolution of, with a minimum of 3

Cylinder

A cylinder that you can change the resolution of, with a minimum of 3

Torus

A donut that you can change the resolution of, with a minimum of 3. We don't recommend eating it through the screen; it won't end well.
