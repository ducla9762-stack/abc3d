Camera Movement

Clicking on the camera icon, the menu, or the 'C' key will bring up the camera panel on the bottom right

Orbit Mode

This is the default mode the program starts in. Left-clicking and dragging the camera will shift in the direction your mouse is going, while the middle mouse wheel will zoom you in and out
The xyz boxes will let you adjust the camera posistion in each respective direction along an increment
Switching to free mode will keep your camera position saved for when you come back/p>

Free Mode

This mode lets you rotate the camera unrestricted by left-clicking and dragging. The middle mouse wheel will move the camera up and down in this mode
The camera will always start where you were in orbit mode, then jump back to that point when you switch back

Vertices & Grid

Always showing vertices with display the vertices regardless of element selected

Toggling 'Always Show Grid' turns the grid on and off. It always starts on.
