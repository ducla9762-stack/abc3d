Controls

Panels & Buttons

Most buttons on the left toolbar have respective panels, pulled up by clicking the button. They give you more information, options, and control.

Gizmos & Sliders

Move, scale, and rotate all have gizmos you can use to assist their respective translations. The grey cube in the center goes along xyz when dragged, while each of the colored ones go along the direction they're facing.
These changes are reflected in the sliders, which do the same thing when you type an input or use the arrows

Keyboard Shortcuts

(Almost) every toolbar button, element selection type, and menu item is bound to a key press. These are also included in the tooltips
The elements are the 1, 2, 3, and 4 number keys
'V' to select, 'C' for camera, 'T' for move, 'S' for scale, 'R' for rotate, 'Q' for shapes, 'X' for extrude, 'Z' for subdivide, 'B' for bevel, 'K' for knife,
'Delete' for deletion, 'CTRL+D' to duplicate, 'CTRL+S' to save
