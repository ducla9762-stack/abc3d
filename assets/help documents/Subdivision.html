Subdivision & Friends:

Tesselation

Splits a face or object into more faces
Used to make higher resolution meshes

Subdivision

Splits an object into more faces and smooths
Used to make higher resolution meshes

Triangulation

Splits a face or object into triangles

Decimation

Reduces an object to fewer triangles while keeping its shape
Set how much to keep with the "Keep %" box, then press Decimate
Open edges (holes and borders) are kept in place
Large objects can take a while, so from about the size of a cube subdivided four times you are shown a time estimate and asked to confirm
The result is all triangles, so quad edge loops are lost and Loop Cut stops at the new triangles
